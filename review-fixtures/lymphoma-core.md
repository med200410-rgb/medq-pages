# MedQ reviewer fixture: Lymphoma core concepts

This short, original teaching fixture is for testing the MedQ exam workflow only.

- Classical Hodgkin lymphoma contains Reed-Sternberg cells. Typical immunophenotype: CD15 positive and CD30 positive; CD45 is usually negative and CD20 is usually negative.
- Nodular sclerosis classical Hodgkin lymphoma often shows broad collagen bands dividing tissue into nodules and lacunar-type Reed-Sternberg cells. Mediastinal involvement is common.
- Follicular lymphoma is commonly associated with t(14;18), leading to BCL2 overexpression. An asymptomatic patient with low tumour burden may be managed initially with watchful waiting.
- Burkitt lymphoma is associated with MYC rearrangement, classically t(8;14), and often has a starry-sky histological appearance. Tumour lysis risk should be assessed before intensive treatment; hydration and urate-lowering prophylaxis are used according to risk.
- Mantle cell lymphoma is associated with t(11;14) and cyclin D1 overexpression.
- Gastric MALT lymphoma associated with Helicobacter pylori may respond to H. pylori eradication therapy.
- The International Prognostic Index for aggressive non-Hodgkin lymphoma includes age over 60 years, stage III/IV, elevated LDH, ECOG performance status greater than 1, and more than one extranodal site.
- Diffuse large B-cell lymphoma is an aggressive B-cell lymphoma.
