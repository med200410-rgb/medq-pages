# MedQ reviewer fixture: ECG basics

This short, original teaching fixture is for testing multiple-file coverage.

- Standard ECG paper speed is 25 mm/s unless otherwise stated.
- One small horizontal square represents 0.04 seconds; one large square represents 0.20 seconds.
- One small vertical square represents 0.1 mV at standard calibration of 10 mm/mV.
- A normal sinus rhythm has a P wave before every QRS complex, each P wave is followed by a QRS, and the rhythm is regular with a rate typically 60-100 beats/minute in adults.
- Lead II commonly shows upright P waves in sinus rhythm.
- The PR interval is measured from the start of the P wave to the start of the QRS complex. A usual adult PR interval is 0.12-0.20 seconds.
- The QRS duration is usually less than 0.12 seconds.
- Atrial fibrillation is characterized by absent discrete P waves and an irregularly irregular ventricular rhythm.
- A regular narrow-complex tachycardia may be caused by AVNRT or AVRT; clinical context and ECG features are needed to distinguish them.
