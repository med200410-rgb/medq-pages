MedQ | Medical MCQ Builder

MedQ helps medical students turn their uploaded lecture materials into source-grounded, difficult board-style multiple-choice practice exams inside ChatGPT.

  • Five distinct answer options (A–E).
  • One unanswered question at a time.
  • Feedback after the student answers.
  • Questions grounded in the materials supplied by the student.

MedQ is currently free to use as provided. Access to ChatGPT may be subject to OpenAI account and plan requirements. Any future paid MedQ features will be clearly identified before they are offered.

MedQ is an independent project developed by ABBAS MUSHTAQ ALI ALHATEMI. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by OpenAI.

For educational practice only. Generated questions and explanations may be incomplete or incorrect; verify important information against your course materials and authoritative references. Do not upload identifiable patient information or confidential records.
