How MedQ operates

MedQ is a skills-only educational ChatGPT plugin. The MedQ team does not operate any external server, file storage, database, or analytics service that collects students' uploaded lectures, prompts, answers or quiz scores. Interactions occur in students' own ChatGPT sessions.

ChatGPT data handling

Uploaded files and conversations are processed by OpenAI according to its applicable policies and account controls; MedQ cannot control how OpenAI stores, uses or deletes that information. Read OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

Public feedback

Information submitted voluntarily to GitHub Issues may be publicly visible and is handled according to GitHub's policies. Never post patient data, passwords, or confidential lecture materials.

Changes and support

The MedQ team will update this policy if its features or data handling change. Contact: support page.
