Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains how the MedQ | Medical MCQ Builder project handles information.

How MedQ works

MedQ is a skills-only educational plugin for ChatGPT. The MedQ publisher does not operate a MedQ backend, API, user account system, database, analytics service, or separate file-storage service. The plugin instructions do not send uploaded lectures, prompts, answers, or quiz scores to the MedQ publisher. The publisher does not receive or store those materials through the plugin.

ChatGPT and uploaded materials

When you use MedQ, the files and messages you submit are processed within your ChatGPT experience. Their handling, storage, and deletion are governed by OpenAI's applicable policies and the controls available in your ChatGPT account. The MedQ publisher cannot access or delete content held in your ChatGPT account.

Read OpenAI's Privacy Policy for information about OpenAI's data practices.

MedQ website

The MedQ information website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about website requests under its own privacy policy. Read GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Optional support and community links

If you choose to visit or contact the MedQ Telegram channel, Telegram processes information according to its own policies. If you submit feedback through GitHub, that content may be publicly visible and is handled by GitHub. Do not send patient-identifiable information, private lecture files, passwords, or confidential records through public support channels.

Telegram: MedQ support and community channel.

Retention, deletion, and sharing

The MedQ publisher does not retain copies of lecture files, prompts, answers, or scores through the plugin and does not sell or share such content. To manage ChatGPT conversations or uploaded files, use the controls provided by ChatGPT. Information submitted directly to GitHub or Telegram is subject to those services' retention and deletion processes.

Children

MedQ is intended for medical students and adult learners, not children under 13. Do not submit a child's personal information.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if MedQ's functionality or data practices change. For questions, use the support page.
